## 8. FAQ answers (common platform questions)

### "How far in advance should we book?"
> Most couples book us 6–12 months in advance for prime wedding dates (May–October Saturdays). Off-peak dates and weekday events can often be booked with 1–3 months' notice. We do accept short-notice bookings when our roster has availability — reach out and we'll let you know within a day.

### "Do you offer a free consultation?"
> Yes. Every booking begins with a free 15–30 minute call (phone or Zoom) where we learn about your event, your venue, your guests, and your musical taste. We use that conversation to recommend the right ensemble and to draft a tailored proposal at no charge.

### "What's included in your pricing?"
> Our quoted price includes the musicians' performance fee, all necessary instruments, sound equipment for the room size, set-up and breakdown, and our coordination throughout the booking process. Standard travel within 25 miles of Boston is included. Custom requests (specific repertoire, additional musicians, unusual venues) are quoted individually.

### "Can you learn special songs for our ceremony / first dance?"
> Yes. We accommodate custom requests as part of every booking. For ceremonies, we'll learn your processional, recessional, and unity-moment selections — at no additional charge for standard requests. For special arrangements (orchestrated covers, unusual instrumentation), we'll quote any added rehearsal time before confirming.

### "What if our musician gets sick or has an emergency?"
> Our 200+ musician roster is our biggest advantage. If a booked musician is unavailable for any reason, we substitute a similarly qualified artist at no additional cost to you. We've never failed to deliver on a confirmed booking.

### "Do you handle outdoor weddings?"
> Yes — we play outdoor ceremonies, garden cocktail hours, tented receptions, and rooftop events regularly. We bring appropriate sound reinforcement, weather-protected instruments where needed, and we'll coordinate with your venue's layout in advance. (Note: we do require a backup indoor location or covered area for instruments in case of weather.)

### "Do you provide a DJ AND live musicians for the same event?"
> Yes, and it's one of our most popular bookings. The Grand Wedding Experience combines string musicians for the ceremony, a jazz trio for cocktail hour, and a DJ for the reception — designed and coordinated as one seamless event. Starting at $4,500.

### "What kinds of events have you played?"
> Weddings of every style — classical, contemporary, intercultural, intimate, large-scale. Hotel residencies and lobby programs. Corporate galas, donor dinners, holiday parties. B'nai mitzvah celebrations. Senior community residencies. Private dinners, anniversary parties, milestone birthdays. Memorial services and funeral receptions.

### "How do we book?"
> 1. Reach out via our website or this platform.
> 2. We schedule a free 15-minute consultation call.
> 3. You receive a tailored proposal with musician options and pricing within 24–48 hours.
> 4. You sign a simple contract, send a 25% deposit to secure the date, and we handle the rest.

## 12. Platform-specific notes

### The Knot
- **Most important platform — largest wedding audience.** Prioritize.
- Has a "Top Pick" badge system you earn through reviews + responsiveness — respond to inquiries within 4 hours.
- Allows full vendor profile with prices, photos, FAQs.
- **Linking landing page:** use `xhojamusicagency.com/wedding-music-boston` as your primary website link.
- Categories: pick "Wedding Bands" and/or "Ceremony Musicians" + "DJs" as your primary tags.

### WeddingWire
- **Shares login with The Knot** (same parent company — The Knot Worldwide). Sign up via The Knot first, then activate WeddingWire from the same dashboard.
- Profile syncs over automatically — you may need to re-upload photos.
- **Same landing page link as The Knot.**

### GigSalad
- Different audience — not just weddings. Corporate events, private parties, school events, etc.
- **Linking landing page:** use `xhojamusicagency.com/private-event-music-boston` here (broader event focus).
- Has a quote-request system. Set up auto-responder if possible.

### Zola
- Smaller wedding platform but growing fast with younger couples.
- Heavier on aesthetic / "vibes" — make sure your photo selection leans modern, warm.
- **Linking landing page:** use `xhojamusicagency.com/wedding-music-boston`.

### The Bash
- Older platform, less Gen Z presence, more legacy event bookers (corporate, schools, etc.).
- Quote-based system — they take a commission, so factor that into pricing (typically 15%).
- **Linking landing page:** use `xhojamusicagency.com/private-event-music-boston`.
- Optional — only sign up if you have capacity for another platform's inquiries.
